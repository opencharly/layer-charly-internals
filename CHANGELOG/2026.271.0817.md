## Summary

Adds **B2c — the PR backlog sweep (org-wide triage)** to the `git-workflow` skill's
`references/multi-repo-coordination.md` (the B2b coordination reference).

The canon already owns each **piece** of a backlog sweep — B2b (cross-session coordination),
`references/watch-and-wake.md` (arm → wake → re-arm), and `references/validator-and-calver.md`
("Replacing a PR"). What was missing is the **org-wide triage runbook** that composes them:
enumerate all open PRs, classify each (superseded / actively worked / stalled), close the
superseded, and start + arm the formal takeover for the stalled. B2c cross-references those
owners rather than restating them.

The load-bearing rule B2c encodes: **triage from LIVE state, never from a thread's claim** — a
"blocked" PR's blocker may already have landed, and a "superseded" PR may never have been closed.
