  verb reaps (merged-upstream worktrees + branches across the umbrella AND every
  submodule), and its **fail-safe contract** — a branch is pruned only when its
  merge is PROVEN (tip is an ancestor of `origin/main`, or its PR is MERGED and its
  tip is contained in the merged head — the squash-merge case git ancestry alone
  cannot see); unmerged commits beyond a merged head are never touched; if `gh` is
  unavailable it prunes strictly LESS; `MODE=report` (default) is a dry run.
- **B8** gains step 8: after the umbrella close-out, run `charly task prune` to
  reap what the per-session steps missed.
