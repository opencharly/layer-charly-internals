# 2026.262.1714 — docs(git-workflow): correct the validator re-trigger mechanics + add the PR-state watcher

## Summary of changes

R1: the git-workflow skill carried **mutually contradictory and false** claims about validator re-triggering, established by direct experiment while landing `spec#137` / `plugin-vm#39` / `plugin-migrate#8`.

**Corrections (each measured):**

- **"An empty commit does NOT re-trigger `pr-validator`"** (the pinned corpus SKILL.md) is **FALSE**. `plugin-pipeline#28` `be3ab30e6` is tree-identical to its parent and fired a fresh `ev=pull_request` run; no dispatcher has a `paths:`/diff guard.
- **"the reusable workflow now re-dispatches itself on the PR head ref (self-heal)"** is **FALSE in both source and pinned**. The reusable workflow's `head_ref` output is never consumed (`grep -c 'steps.pr.outputs'` = 0); no dispatcher re-dispatches itself. A `workflow_dispatch` **requires `--ref`**.
- **"the workflow now dedupes ... duplicate same-name check runs can no longer poison mergeability"** is **FALSE as an org-wide claim**: per-repo OPT-IN (3 of the ~400 submodule repos); the reusable workflow deliberately has none.

**What replaces them — the BODY-BEFORE-PUSH RULE (the operator's emphasis):** finish ALL PR-body text BEFORE the push the validator will review (it reads the body at review time, keys its verdict to the head it sees). Measured: `plugin-vm#39`'s auto run reviewed the pre-edit body → BLOCK; a re-dispatch after the edit → PASS on the SAME head. Also documents the **POISON state** (a green re-dispatch stuck behind an older same-name FAILURE) and the `pr_state_watch.sh` poll.

**AGENTS.md alignment:** adds a `references/umbrella-mechanics` entity (the umbrella dispatcher routes its pinning row to this skill), plus a section labelling those commands as **umbrella-repo maintenance**.

**Files:** `charly.yml` (+223/−34, 1 file). The added/changed regions, by entity:

- `git-workflow-skill` → its **top-level `content:`** — the corrected re-trigger section, the `## The PR-state watcher` section, and the cross-reference pointer.
- `git-workflow-skill` → the **`validator-and-calver` reference entity's `content:`** — the empty-commit re-freeze note (after "If validation FAILS or R10 fails") plus the entire `## The POISON state` section. (This is the ~46-line region the previous body mis-attributed to the top-level `content:`.)
- `git-workflow-skill` → the **`references:` list** — a new `umbrella-mechanics` entry (a sibling of `validator-and-calver`), plus its entry in the reference-index table.
